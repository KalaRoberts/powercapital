Is it time?

Common signs you're ready for a fractional CFO.

Capital Draw Schedules and Cash Flow Gaps Are Outpacing Forecasting

If cash flow management feels like a reactive scramble—where funding construction milestones, vendor invoices, or contractor pay applications consistently bumps up against delayed financing tranches or working capital squeezes—you lack the forward-looking liquidity modeling required for heavy infrastructure.

Complex Debt Stacks and Investor Structures Require Rigorous Modeling

When institutional investors, lenders, or board members ask granular questions about debt service coverage ratios (DSCR), capital stack optimization, internal rates of return (IRR) across different phases, or scenario stress-testing, and your internal team cannot produce institutional-grade financial models on demand.

Job-Level Cost Overruns and Scope Creep Are Eroding Margins Silently

You know top-line project volume is high, but true, project-by-project profitability remains murky until the build is fully closed out. If cost overruns or unrecovered change orders are discovered too late to correct—and you lack real-time tracking of committed costs versus actuals—your gross margins are at high risk.

Leadership Is Bogged Down in Financial Structuring Instead of Growth

The founders, managing directors, or technical leads are spending their days buried in complex financial spreadsheets, trying to untangle tax implications, or manually structuring project pro formas instead of focusing on strategic pipeline expansion and site acquisition.

Earlier is usually better than later.
