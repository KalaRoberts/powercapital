Meet our President

A finance partner forged in Amazon's hypergrowth — now bringing that discipline to growing companies as their fractional CFO.

Where it began

John Roberts is a trusted finance partner with 16 years as a finance professional — including 15 years solving large-scale and highly ambiguous problems at AWS and Amazon.com.

He has a proven ability to scale alongside Amazon during extreme growth — a period in which the company's market capitalization increased 4,241%, from $56.9B to $2.47T. That environment demands rigor, speed, and judgment under uncertainty, and it's the foundation John brings to every engagement.

Today, based in Raleigh, North Carolina, John is President of Power Capital Consulting, partnering with hyperscale data-center developers and cloud-infrastructure investors to sharpen their financial and accounting processes — and bringing that same CFO-level discipline to growing companies.

  • Financial system design
  • Data-center & server infrastructure finance
  • Supply chain & operations finance
  • Full P&L ownership of products
  • Forecasting & financial operations
  • Financial planning & analysis (FP&A)

Career history

Sixteen years — billions in scope.

From Amazon to Fractional CFO.

2025 – Present

President — Power Capital Consulting

Partners with hyperscale data-center developers (PowerHouse) and cloud-infrastructure investors — designing compliance and audit functions for development costs, leading real-estate development software implementation (Northspyre), and driving scalable, profitable process improvement.

2024 – 2025

AWS Accounting Consultant

Led cross-functional automation projects with CFO sponsorship — full data-center asset-lifecycle automation, controllership for a new power-generator manufacturing process, and accelerating construction timelines for AI-driven mega-campus demand.

2022 – 2024

Senior Finance Manager — AWS Infrastructure Finance (BPM)

Liaison between Finance, Accounting, and technical teams on large-scale projects — for scale, AWS held $108.5B in assets and $24.8B in net PP&E additions in 2023. Exceeded the CFO's goal for fixed-asset PO-coding accuracy with a solution still in use years later.

2019 – 2022

Senior Finance Manager — AWS Data Center Infrastructure

Led the finance team for data-center capex — growing from $1.5B to $3B/yr on AWS's largest commercial cluster, then scaling to lead global DC capex of $12B/yr with a team of five.

2018 – 2019

Product CFO — AWS Networking (ELB, NAT Gateway, PrivateLink, EC2)

Full P&L ownership for multiple services generating over $1B in revenue — pricing strategy, multi-year forecasting, and cost-allocation methodology.

2015 – 2018

Finance Manager — AWS Server Infrastructure, Supply Chain & Procurement

Led a team of five supporting multi-billion-dollar server procurement and supply chain (incl. Annapurna) — modeling a multi-billion-dollar server-manufacturing initiative and leading multi-generational CPU pricing negotiations with Intel and others.

2013 – 2015

Financial Analyst II — AWS Central FP&A

Built the automated Weekly Business Review consolidating inputs from thirty-five teams for senior executives — including then-AWS-CEO Andy Jassy.

2012 – 2013

Financial Analyst II — Amazon Accounts Receivable, Business Intelligence

Led the establishment of a global Business Intelligence team, delivering online analytic dashboards and ad hoc reporting across nine countries — driving data-driven insights, operational efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

2010 – 2012

Financial Analyst — Amazon Accounts Receivable, Vendor Dispute & Operations

Led the development and implementation of scalable best practices for processing vendor disputes, managing the transition of vendor dispute work to an offshore team in India — overseeing training, escalations, and work quality for US-based contractors and the Hyderabad team.
