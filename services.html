Core services

What we deliver.

Internal Audit & Controls

Framework to pinpoint missing controls, redundant processes, or compliance risk — making you audit-ready.

Financial Systems & FP&A

Designing the reporting infrastructure and FP&A cadence that let you trust the numbers and plan ahead with confidence.

Budgets & Burn-Rate Control

An operating budget you actually use, budget-versus-actual reviews, and spending guardrails that protect your runway.

Financial Models & Business Plans

Data-driven plans for growth, pricing, new locations, or product lines — with realistic revenue assumptions you can defend.

Capital Raising

Investor-ready financials, pitch-deck numbers, lender materials, SBA and bank loan preparation, and support through due diligence.

KPI Dashboards & Board Reporting

Key performance indicators that matter for your business.

Accounting Policy & Governance

Compliance policies across heavy CapEx capitalization rules, lease accounting, and revenue recognition, ensuring institutional audit readiness and protecting capital deployment.

Cash Flow Forecasting

Rolling 13-week and 12-month forecasts so you see a cash gap months before it arrives, with a concrete plan to close it.
