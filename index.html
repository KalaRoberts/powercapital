The build is scaling — and the finance function can't keep pace.

Data center builds involve intricate debt stacks, construction loans, and strict financial covenants. Without rigorous internal controls and audit-ready reporting, a single missed metric or mismanaged draw schedule can trigger a default or stall a multi-million-dollar build.

Draw schedules outpace your forecast

Funding construction milestones, vendor invoices, and contractor pay applications keeps colliding with delayed financing tranches — and you're managing liquidity reactively instead of modeling it ahead.

The capital stack demands real modeling

Lenders and investors ask about DSCR, capital-stack optimization, and phase-level IRR — and your team can't produce institutional-grade models and stress tests on demand.

Job-level margins stay murky

Project volume is high, but true profitability isn't clear until a build closes out. Cost overruns and unrecovered change orders surface too late to fix, with no real-time committed-vs-actual tracking.

The Power Capital difference

Heavy capital demands absolute precision.

Power Capital provides executive financial architecture for data centers, construction, and real estate—protecting high-stakes builds from draw friction and compliance gridlock.
